5. Classification-of-Rice-Varieties-Machine-Learning
This study has been focused on classification of five rice varieties namely Arborio, Basmati, Ipsala, Jasmine,
and Karacadag using machine learning techniques.
 
• Classification models such as k-Nearest Neighbor (KNN), Multi Layered Perceptron (MLP) and Support Vector Machine (SVM) are used for the classification
of five rice varieties.
• The study aims to demonstrate that integrating morphological, shape and color features can enhance
classification accuracy.
• Average Accuracy of 99.86% was obtained and major reduction of features (from base-paper) was done, from
which the training time was reduced by 46.00% and space complexity was reduced by 54.00%

9. CTCVR-Prediction-for-BBA-Buyer-Book-Activity
CTCVR Prediction via FM+DNN+BBA2vec (Python, NumPy, scikit-learn, LightGBM, SHAP)
Reproduced and extended a dual-target e-commerce conversion rate prediction framework (Wei et al., Expert Systems with Applications, 2024). Built a logit-calibrated synthetic dataset of 50,000 interaction records across 3,000 users, 5,000 products, and 200 campaigns. Implemented a custom Factorisation Machine (FM) with mini-batch SGD, a two-hidden-layer MLP with positive-class oversampling, and extended BBA2vec graph embeddings (48-dim) from a tripartite buyer-book-activity graph. An 8-configuration ablation study identified gap-closure feature engineering as the highest-impact component (+0.53% AUC). Final model achieves AUC = 0.8126, ECE = 0.049, and 95% bootstrap CI [0.768, 0.812]. Findings published as a full research paper with ablation tables, SHAP beeswarm plots, and calibration curves.
